Your data

Do I need an account?

No. EpicLifts has no accounts or sign-up. Open it and start lifting.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone, encrypted. We never receive it. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

An iCloud backup of your iPhone (or an encrypted backup on your computer) includes it. You can also use Settings › Back Up Now to save a backup file, then Settings › Restore from Backup on the new phone.

I deleted the app and lost my data.

Your data lives only in the app, so deleting it removes everything. Restore from a backup file or an iPhone backup.

Getting started

Can I bring my history from another app?

Yes. Export a CSV from your old app, then use Settings › Import from Another App. It reads workouts, food diaries and weigh-ins.

Does it work offline?

Yes, completely. Only food barcode and search lookups for foods not already on your phone, and recipe links, need a connection.

Apple Watch and Health

The watch app doesn't show heart rate.

Open EpicLifts on your watch once and allow Health access. Heart rate and calories appear once a workout starts.

How do I turn on Apple Health sync?

In the app, go to Settings › Apple Health and choose what to sync. You can change Health permissions any time in the Health app.
