Support

Found a bug or have an idea? We'd love to hear it.

Contact us: open a support request. Please don't include personal health details.

Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. EpicLifts has no accounts or sign-up.

Where is my data?
On your iPhone, encrypted. We never receive it. See the privacy policy.

How do I keep my data when I change phones?
An iCloud backup (or an encrypted backup on your computer) of your iPhone includes it. You can also use Settings › Back Up Now to save a backup file, then Settings › Restore from Backup on the new phone.

Deleting the app deleted my data.
Data lives only in the app, so deleting it removes everything. Restore from a backup file or an iPhone backup.

Can I bring my history from another app?
Yes. Export a CSV from your old app and use Settings › Import from Another App. It reads workouts, food diaries and weigh-ins.

The Apple Watch app doesn't show heart rate.
Open EpicLifts on the watch once and allow Health access. Heart rate and calories appear once a workout starts.
