Privacy

Your data stays on your iPhone.

We built EpicLifts so we never see your workouts, food or health data. Here's the short version, then the details.

✓No accountNothing to sign up for
✓No ads or trackingNo analytics, ever
✓EncryptedStored on your device
✓Nothing sent to usWe run no servers

What we collect

Nothing. We never receive your workouts, food diary, bodyweight, measurements, health data or any other personal information.

Where your data lives

On your iPhone, encrypted

Everything you log is kept in an encrypted database on your device. Its key is stored in the iOS Keychain.

Your Apple Watch

If you use the watch app, it records heart rate and calories during a workout and sends them only to the app on your iPhone.

Apple Health optional

Off until you turn it on. With your permission, EpicLifts reads your weigh-ins and saves workouts, bodyweight and nutrition to Apple Health. Health data is never used for ads, never shared and never leaves your device through us.

Backups you make

Settings › Back Up Now creates a file you choose where to save or send. We never see it.

Deleting the app deletes its data, so keep a backup if you want to restore it later.

When the app goes online

Only for things you ask for, and only with what's needed:

Food barcode and search

For a food that isn't on your phone yet, the barcode or the words you typed go to Open Food Facts, an open food database. Nothing else about you is sent. Product photos load from there too, under its own privacy policy.

Recipes from a link

When you paste or open a recipe link, the app loads that page, like a browser would.

App updates

The app checks Expo, our update service, for fixes and improvements. That check includes the app version and device platform, not your data.

Camera, photos and notifications

Camera

Used only to scan barcodes. Nothing is recorded or saved.

Screenshots

Screenshots you pick for a recipe are read on your iPhone with Apple's on-device text recognition and are never uploaded.

Notifications

Rest timer alerts and reminders are scheduled on your device. No notification service of ours is involved.

Purchases

If you buy EpicLifts Pro, Apple handles the payment. We never get your payment details, and your purchase is checked on your device.

The fine print

Children. EpicLifts isn't directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
