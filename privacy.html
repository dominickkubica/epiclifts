Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

EpicLifts is built so your training and food data stay on your iPhone. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and we run no servers that receive your data.

What we collect

Nothing. We (the developer) never receive your workouts, food diary, bodyweight, measurements, health data or any other personal information.

Where your data lives

  • On your iPhone, encrypted. Everything you log is stored in an encrypted database on your device. Its key is kept in the iOS Keychain.
  • Your Apple Watch. If you use the watch app, it records heart rate and calories during a workout and sends them to the app on your iPhone. They aren't sent anywhere else.
  • Apple Health (optional, off until you turn it on). With your permission, EpicLifts can read your weigh-ins and write workouts, bodyweight and nutrition to Apple Health. Health data is never used for advertising, never shared with anyone else and never leaves your device through us.
  • Backups you make. Settings › Back Up Now creates a file you choose where to save or send. We never see it.

Deleting the app deletes its data. Keep a backup if you want to restore it later.

When the app uses the internet

Only for things you ask for, and only with the minimum information needed:

  • Food barcode and search lookups. When you scan a barcode or search for a food that isn't already on your phone, the barcode or the words you typed go to Open Food Facts, an open food database. Nothing else about you is sent. Product photos load from Open Food Facts too. Its own privacy policy applies to those requests.
  • Recipes from a link. When you paste or open a recipe link, the app loads that web page, like a browser would.
  • App updates. The app checks Expo (our update service) for bug fixes and improvements. That check includes the app version and device platform, not your data.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only to scan barcodes, and nothing is recorded or saved. Screenshots you pick for a recipe are read on your iPhone with Apple's on-device text recognition and are not uploaded.

Notifications

Rest timer alerts and reminders are scheduled on your device. No notification service of ours is involved.

Purchases

If you buy EpicLifts Pro, Apple handles the payment. We get no payment details, and your purchase is checked on your device.

Children

EpicLifts is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
