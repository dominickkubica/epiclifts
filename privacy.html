Privacy Policy

Your data stays on your iPhone.

EpicLifts is designed so that we never receive your training, nutrition or health information. This policy explains, in full, what the app does with your information and the limited cases in which it connects to the internet.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the EpicLifts app for iPhone and Apple Watch (the "App") and to this website. "We," "us" and "our" refer to the developer of EpicLifts. By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. We do not require an account, and we do not receive, store, sell, rent or share your workouts, food diary, bodyweight, body measurements, notes, health data, photos, contacts, location, device identifiers or any other personal information. The App contains no third-party analytics, advertising, attribution or tracking software, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

3. Information stored on your device

Everything you enter in the App is stored locally and remains under your control:

Encrypted local storage

Your data is kept in an encrypted database on your iPhone. The encryption key is held in the iOS Keychain and never leaves your device through us.

Apple Watch

If you use the watch app, it records heart rate and active calories during a workout using Apple HealthKit and sends them directly to the App on your paired iPhone. This information is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Apple Health optional

Health integration is off until you enable it. With your explicit permission, the App may read your weigh-ins and write workouts, bodyweight and nutrition to Apple Health. Information obtained through HealthKit is used solely to provide these features. It is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, never shared with third parties and never transmitted to us. You can change these permissions at any time in the Health app or in iOS Settings.

Backups and exports

When you choose Back Up Now or export your data, the App creates a file and lets you decide where to save or send it. We do not receive these files.

Device backups

Like other app data, your information may be included in iCloud or computer backups of your iPhone, under your Apple account and Apple's terms.

4. When the App connects to the internet

The App works fully offline. It connects to the internet only for the following features, and sends only the minimum information each one requires. As with any internet connection, the service you reach receives your device's IP address as a technical necessity. We do not receive it.

Food barcode and search lookups

When you scan a barcode or search for a food that is not already on your device, the barcode number or the search words you typed are sent to Open Food Facts, an open, nonprofit food database. Product images are loaded from Open Food Facts as well. No other information about you is sent. Results you choose are saved on your device so they work offline afterwards. Open Food Facts' own privacy policy governs these requests.

Recipes from a link

When you paste or open a recipe link, the App loads that web page much as a browser would. The website you visit may receive standard browser information, under its own privacy policy.

App updates

The App checks Expo, our update service, for bug fixes and improvements. These requests include technical details such as the app version, update channel and device platform. They contain none of your personal data.

5. Device permissions

The App asks for permission before using any of the following, and you may decline or revoke access at any time in iOS Settings:

Camera

Used only to scan food barcodes. Images are processed in real time and are not recorded or saved.

Photos

Used only to read screenshots you select for a recipe. Text is recognized on your iPhone with Apple's on-device technology, and images are never uploaded.

Notifications

Used for rest timer alerts and optional reminders, which are scheduled on your device. We operate no notification service.

Health

Used only as described in section 3.

6. Apple and purchases

The App is distributed through Apple's App Store. Downloads, payments and any in-app purchases, such as EpicLifts Pro, are processed by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We never receive your payment details, and purchase verification happens on your device.

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may provide us with aggregated, de-identified statistics and crash reports. These reports do not identify you, and you can turn this sharing off at any time under Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

7. Data security

Your data is protected by encryption on your device, by your iPhone's own security (such as your passcode, Face ID or Touch ID), and by the iOS Keychain. Because we do not hold your data, it cannot be exposed through a breach of our systems. No method of storage is completely secure, so we recommend keeping your iPhone's software up to date and protecting it with a passcode.

8. Retention and deletion

Your information remains on your device for as long as you keep it there. You can edit or delete individual entries in the App at any time. Deleting the App permanently removes its data from your iPhone. Copies in backups or export files you created are removed when you delete those backups or files.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA, as amended by the CPRA) give you rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your personal information, and to object to certain processing. Because we do not collect or hold your personal information, you can exercise these rights directly in the App: view and edit everything you have logged, export it at any time, or delete it. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under California law, and we do not use it for targeted advertising or profiling.

If you have a question or concern about your rights, please contact us using the details below. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

10. International users

EpicLifts can be used worldwide. Because your information stays on your device, we do not transfer it internationally. Requests made to the third-party services described in section 4 are handled by those services under their own policies.

11. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, or under the age of digital consent in your country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App evolves or as laws change. When we do, we will post the revised version on this page and update the effective date above. If a change materially affects how your information is handled, we will provide notice in the App before it takes effect.
